# AI & Deep Learning — Handwritten Study Notes

A collection of handwritten notes covering the foundations of **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL)**, along with the mathematics and neural-network concepts used to understand how models learn.

The PDF brings together conceptual notes, equations, diagrams, and practical Keras examples. It is intended as a learning and revision resource for beginners building a foundation in machine learning and deep learning.

## 📄 Notes

**PDF:** [`ai_DL_.pdf`](ai_DL_.pdf)

Open the PDF above to read the original handwritten notes.

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. AI, Machine Learning & Deep Learning
- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
- Relationship between AI, ML, and DL
- Supervised and unsupervised learning
- Examples and applications of AI, including computer vision and natural language processing
- Deep-learning use cases and model families

### 2. Neural Network Foundations
- Artificial neurons and the perceptron
- Weights, bias, and neuron output
- Forward propagation
- Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)
- Dense layers and the basic structure of a neural network

### 3. Mathematical Foundations
- Scalars and vectors
- Vector magnitude and direction
- Differentiation and derivatives
- Rate of change and slope
- Maxima and minima
- Gradients and the chain rule

These mathematical ideas help explain how a neural network measures error and adjusts its parameters during training.

### 4. Training Neural Networks
- Loss/error and model predictions
- Backpropagation
- Gradient calculation
- Weight updates
- Gradient Descent and Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)
- Batch-related training concepts, including batch normalization

### 5. Activation Functions
The notes discuss why activation functions are used and how they affect neural-network learning, including:
- Sigmoid
- Tanh
- ReLU
- Leaky ReLU
- Softmax
- Linear activation

They also cover where activation functions are commonly used, such as hidden layers, binary classification, multiclass classification, and regression output layers.

### 6. Weight Initialization
- Why neural-network weights need suitable initial values
- Xavier/Glorot initialization
- He initialization
- Initialization and its relationship to activation functions and gradient flow

### 7. Keras / Model-Building Basics
- Building a sequential neural-network model
- Adding Dense layers and choosing units/activations
- Compiling a model
- Selecting loss functions and optimizers
- Metrics, validation, batch size, and epochs
- Training and evaluating a model

The PDF includes handwritten code-oriented examples and diagrams to support the concepts.

## 🎯 Learning Goals

These notes are useful for:
- Building foundational knowledge of AI and deep learning
- Revising neural-network concepts
- Understanding the mathematics behind backpropagation and optimization
- Preparing for entry-level Data Science and Machine Learning interviews
- Reviewing common activation and weight-initialization techniques
